Eden Cinema
Visual Identity
A new visual identity for Eden, the first cinema in Tel Aviv, reimagined as a contemporary home for Israeli film and local creation.
The project brings Eden back to life as a platform for emerging filmmakers, with all revenue reinvested in supporting young creators and Israeli cinema. Created as my graduation project in Visual Communication at HIT.
