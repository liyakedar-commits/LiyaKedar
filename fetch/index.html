Fetch is a magazine inspired by the 2000s, exploring the quirks of popular culture. The “Guilty Pleasures” issue dives into the tension between temptation, joy, and shame, offering a playful yet critical take on the things we secretly love but hesitate to admit. Blending fun and with sharp reflection, it celebrates the contradictions and complexities of our hidden indulgences.
