Here's How It Happened
Title Sequence
A title sequence for a series based on Meir Shalev's book. Here's What Happened is a witty collection of short stories about family life in Nahalal during Israel's pioneering days. Full of secrets and charm, the stories are told with warmth and humor, celebrating the art of storytelling itself - where every tale has more than one version, and the truth? That depends on who's telling it.
