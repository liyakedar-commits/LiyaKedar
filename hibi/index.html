Hibi
Mobile App
A weather app inspired by Ikko Tanaka, built around Japan's 72 micro-seasons. It offers a unique way to experience forecasts, weaving together weather insights with traditions, festivals, and the subtle shifts in nature. Visualizing time, nature & tradition.
I made a set of icons for the seasons and the small things that mark them. Working on them I kept coming back to Ikko Tanaka - simple, basic geometric shapes, and how much they can hold.
